Subscription details.

Do paid benefits apply to ranked events?

Yes. Score multipliers, flag reveals and placement guarantees apply to the main event leaderboard. Paid and Free teams compete in the same division.

Can my team use AI or LLM tools?

AI and LLM tools are prohibited on the Free plan, including code assistants, chatbots and agents. Competitive and Premier teams may use them to analyse challenges and generate solutions. Premier also permits autonomous solving agents.

When does an upgrade take effect?

Benefits activate immediately. Existing solves are recalculated using your new score multiplier, including flags obtained through reveals.

Does early access count towards first blood?

Yes. Solves submitted before public release qualify for first-blood bonuses. Free teams receive access at the scheduled event start.

Subscriptions renew monthly. Benefits apply to every member of the subscribed team. Premier placement is reserved before score-based positions are assigned.
